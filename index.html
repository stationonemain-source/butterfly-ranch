From the kill pen to a kid's own horse.

We take in horses from kill pens, abusive homes and surrenders, bring them back to health, and match each one with a student who cares for it all summer. Some go home with that student for good.

GiveVolunteer

A horse that nobody wanted gets fed, shod, doctored and worked, slowly, until a kid shows up and keeps showing up. That's the whole program.

How it works

Rescue. Rehab. Rematch.

Every horse at the ranch is on the same road. Where it is on that road is written up on its own page, so the people who paid for the hay can see what the hay did.

01

Rescue

Horses come to us from dangerous places: kill pens, abusive homes, and owners who can no longer keep them.

02

Rehab

Feed, supplements, farrier, vet and time. The work goes at the horse's pace, not ours.

03

Rematch

When a horse is ready, it meets the students. Each summer a student is assigned a horse to care for, and some become its owner.

The barn

Who's here

Braxton started Butterfly Ranch in 2022 because the community needed a place where kids and adults could spend time with horses and get away from city life.

Braxton Brackin

Founder & CEO

Raised around rodeo and riding for 27 years, Braxton learned calf roping from her uncle before she could walk. She still competes, and teaches with a firm but encouraging style that helps students believe they can do anything they set their minds to.

Anna Foote

Barn Manager & Instructor

Born and raised in Cody, Wyoming, Anna rode six years with a rodeo drill team before moving to Oklahoma after college in 2023. She loves breakaway roping and teaching riders of every age.

Chloe Bjornberg

Instructor

A former president of the OU Equestrian Team, Chloe has taught English and western lessons for over eight years. She's now in graduate school at OU, training to offer equine-assisted counseling.
