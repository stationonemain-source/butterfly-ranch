How a horse comes through

Rescue, rehab, and every step in the open

  1. 1

    Rescue

    Horses come to us from dangerous places: kill pens, abusive homes, and owners who can no longer keep them.

  2. 2

    Rehabilitation

    Feed, supplements, farrier, vet care and time. The work goes at the horse's pace, not ours.

  3. 3

    Followed, in public

    Every step goes on the horse's page: what was done, what it cost and who helped. Donors see exactly where their gift went.
