Questions

Sponsoring, answered

What does a sponsor get?

A photo and a short note each month on the horse you sponsor, your name (or your business's) on its page if you want it there, and an invitation to come out and meet it. Businesses get a logo on this page and a thank-you on the ranch's social channels.

How long does it last?

Month to month. Stop whenever you need to; the horse stays.

Can two people sponsor the same horse?

Yes. Care for one horse costs more than most single sponsorships, so several sponsors on one horse is normal and welcome.

Is it tax-deductible?

Butterfly Rescue Ranch is a 501(c)(3), so yes, to the extent the law allows. You'll get a receipt for each payment.
